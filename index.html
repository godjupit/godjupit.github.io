About

I’m interested in the software layers that make computing systems fast, reliable, and useful.

My projects and learning span systems and infrastructure, high-performance computing, operating systems, and LLM inference. I like to understand a problem from its fundamentals, build a working implementation, and use measurement to guide the next iteration.

This site is a concise record of what I’m exploring. Project descriptions reflect areas of work; links will be added as repositories are ready to share.
